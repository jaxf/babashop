MethodHow move grades workShow

These are current-value estimates, not historical verdicts or NBA projections. Player value blends league-scored weekly production with draft capital, shrinking small samples toward the draft prior. Recent form receives 25% of the production weight. Values subtract an estimated replacement baseline so a pile of bench players does not automatically beat a star.

Balanced trades center on C (70); upgrades score higher. Low- and medium-confidence grades are pulled toward neutral to avoid extreme verdicts on thin evidence. Unknown player values and pick-only deals stay ungraded. Draft picks use a round-based heuristic discounted by years away; FAAB is normalized to the league budget. Injury flags reduce confidence, not an invented recovery forecast. Roster eligibility, games remaining, salaries, and keeper rules are not modeled.
